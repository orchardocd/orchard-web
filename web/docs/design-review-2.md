## 2. Plan

1. **`components/ui/Media.tsx`** — stop `MediaImage` from forcing an upscale: keep `h-auto w-full` but add `style={{ maxWidth: resolved.width }}` so no asset renders wider than its intrinsic width, and give `RoundImage` a uniform disc (`bg-mist ring-1 ring-line grayscale`) so cut-outs, colour photos and placeholders present one edge and one tone.
2. **`components/blocks/RenderBlocks.tsx`** — one pass over the block renderer: drop `mx-auto` at lines 139, 175 and 197 so the measure hangs off the container gutter; drop `items-start` at line 173 and start the portraits grid at `grid-cols-2` (line 176); constrain the logos row (line 154) to `max-w-measure` and give each mark an equal padded cell; cap the lone `imageBlock` figure (lines 79-86) with a bounded plate (`max-h-56 w-auto object-contain`, mist ground for logo and illustration assets, asymmetric `mt-6 mb-2` margins); special-case `cards.length === 1` to a fixed round frame with a centred caption; and style the first caption paragraph as the name (line 188: semibold `text-ink`, affiliation one step smaller).
3. **`components/RichText.tsx`** — rebuild the PROSE ramp: `text-base md:text-lg` base (line 7), h2 up on mobile and set in `text-brand-deep`, h3/h4 dropped to visibly subordinate steps (lines 9-11), `[&_p]:my-6` (line 8), a `[&>p:first-child]` lead step, and a `[&_a[href$='.pdf']]` rule reusing the bordered pill chrome from `DocumentLink.tsx:13` so file links read as downloads everywhere.
4. **`components/ui/Button.tsx` and `components/layout/PageHero.tsx`** — add `border-2 border-transparent` to `BASE` (line 17) so all variants share one box height, then in `HeroHighlights`: `items-stretch` plus `h-full` on the `li` (lines 24-34), `p-6 sm:p-8` padding, card heading down to `text-xl md:text-2xl` (line 43), `md:grid-cols-2` when the set has more than four cards, and a `[&_p:has(>a:only-child)>a]` rule on the card rich text that applies `buttonClasses('secondary')`.

Only their scale is normalised, in item 1 and item 10.
- **Splicing `PeopleSections` in at the in-body headings, and the "stranded roster headings" findings**: already handled. `groupBlocks.ts:100-113` (`isOrphanTitle` fed by `renderedElsewhere` from `[slug]/page.tsx:42`) already removes "Our Team", "Scientific Advisory Board" and "Our Supporters". Only "Our Volunteers" survives, because the NIHR paragraph sits in the same split part, and relocating that image out from under its heading breaks the parity heading-association rule.
- **Re-cropping source assets** (video poster pillarbox, roster face scale and tone, Christine Lochner margins, Hertfordshire and NIHR logo whitespace, the Jim Hagan silhouette): media are mirrored from the old site and keyed by upload path in the parity check. The corrections land in `RoundImage` and the image plates instead.
- **Raising the conference map zoom to z=15-16 in `seed/content.json`**: that file is generated by `tools/extract.py` from the mirror, so a hand edit is overwritten and diverges the seed from its source.
- **Anything that adds wording**: section counters on /the-work-we-do, "load more" or "showing N of 84" labels, rewritten eyebrows, a "Studies you can take part in" style lead-in. Copy is locked by the parity check, which fails any string absent from the mirrored old page. The pagination control in item 11 stays under the 12-character text floor, and the newsletter form in item 9 reuses the old form's own Name / Email / Submit fields.
- **Hiding the "decorative-only" illustration row on mobile /** (the three specks beside "Learn About Orchard OCD") and **suppressing the /about-ocd two-mark strip**: both remove parity-tracked images. Item 2 constrains the row to the measure and caps the mark height instead.
- **A gradient fade under the header hairline**: superseded by making the header opaque and adding `scroll-padding-top` in item 8.
- **Capping every `MediaImage` with a derived max-height**: aspect-boxed usages (`h-full w-full object-cover` in card plates and video facades) must fill their frame. The cap is applied by intrinsic width in item 1 and at the figure level in item 2.
